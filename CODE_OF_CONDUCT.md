# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). The short version is below. The linked text is the full version.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Brio UI to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient with people who are new.
- Respect other opinions and give feedback that helps.
- Own mistakes and learn from them.
- Keep the discussion on the work.

## Not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment in public or in private.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would call unprofessional in this space.

## Enforcement

The maintainers remove, edit or reject comments, issues, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may ban a person for behaviour they judge harmful. This applies in this repo and when someone represents the project in public.

Report a problem by email to hello@brioui.com. Reports are read by the maintainer and handled in private. The reporter's identity stays confidential.
